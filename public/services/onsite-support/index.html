PROVEN FIELD EXECUTION // CASE STUDY

Kissimmee Medical Facility: Emergency Lightning Surge Recovery

The Challenge

A lightning strike at 7:15 AM fried the main UPS battery backup and core access switch at a busy Kissimmee medical center, threatening cancellation of 80 patient appointments.

Engineering Solution

Steve Cormier arrived onsite within 38 minutes carrying a pre-configured enterprise managed switch and commercial online UPS, bypassed the fried gear, patched the core VLANs, and verified electronic health record server connectivity.

Quantified Outcome

All clinical systems returned to 100% operational status by 8:30 AM before the first scheduled patient entered the clinic, preventing tens of thousands of dollars in lost billing.
